How to Join and Play

To join the Tournament follow the steps below

  1. Register or log in to your account.
  2. Create your team and become the team leader.
  3. Add up to four teammates.
  4. Choose your team name and upload a logo.
  5. Enter the in-game name and Game ID of every player.
  6. Only registered players can participate in tournaments.
  7. PC and Console players are currently not eligible.
